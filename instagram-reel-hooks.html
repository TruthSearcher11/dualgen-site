Reels hooks have to work muted and spoken

Can I use these hooks on TikTok too?

Often yes. Switch the platform to TikTok and regenerate if you want the prompt's format (comment prompt vs caption) to match that app.

Will Instagram punish “templated” hooks?

Platforms reward watch time, not whether a first line came from a formula. The risk is sounding generic. Edit until it sounds like you.
