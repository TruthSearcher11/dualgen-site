What a good TikTok hook has to do

On TikTok you have about a second. The hook has to name a person or a problem, create a reason to stay, and sound like something a human would actually say out loud. DualGen writes that opening line first, then builds a prompt that tells a model to keep the hook verbatim and write a 20–40 second script around it, with on-screen text and a comment prompt at the end.

This page is for creators, marketers, and owners who post talking-head or screen-recording videos and are tired of staring at a blank first line. It is a TikTok hook generator and a script prompt generator in one step — the “dual” in DualGen.

How to use the pairs

Pick the hook you would actually say on camera. Don't force a formula that isn't in your mouth. Paste the matching prompt into ChatGPT or Grok, generate a draft, then cut anything that sounds like a template. The prompt already asks for a short script, so you should be editing, not inventing the structure.

If a pair is useful, copy the share link. Anyone who opens it will see the same topic, platform, and that hook + prompt combination.
