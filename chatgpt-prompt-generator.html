Why the prompt is locked to a hook

If you ask ChatGPT to “write a TikTok script about meal prep,” you often get a polite introduction and a hook that arrives too late. DualGen flips the order. You leave with a hook you can already say on camera, and a prompt that tells the model: do not write anything before this line, match this platform and tone, and finish a usable draft.

Pick a platform so the prompt includes the right length and format — a 20-second Reel is not a LinkedIn post. Then paste the prompt as-is, or add your own extra constraints (“no slang,” “include this product name,” “keep it under 80 words”).

What you can use it for

Scripts, captions, threads, explainers, soft-sell offers, and before/after stories. The generator includes frameworks for educate, sell, story, controversy, list, and before/after goals. It is not a substitute for a specialist prompt you have already tuned for a long workflow; it is the fastest way to get a first-line-plus-brief when you need to post today.
