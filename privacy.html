Privacy policy

Last updated November 19, 2026. This policy is written for a free generator and a newsletter signup. It is not formal legal advice.

Who we are

DualGen AI (“DualGen,” “we,” “us”) is a free prompt and hook generator and weekly email pack, operated by Patrick Totten in Quincy, Massachusetts, United States. The website is dualgenai.com.

The generator

The DualGen generator runs entirely in your browser. Your topic, audience, platform, tone, and the pairs you generate are not sent to our server (this site is static). We do not use an AI API when you click Generate. If you copy a share link or share a URL that includes your topic, anyone with that link can see those details. Generation counts used to decide when to highlight the email offer are stored in your browser (localStorage) only.

What we collect if you subscribe

If you join the list, we collect the email address you enter, the time you signed up, and basic technical information your browser or the form provider may send (such as IP address or whether the signup succeeded). We do not ask for a password or payment card on this site.

How we use it

We use your email to send the weekly prompt and hook pack, the 100-hook PDF (or a link to it), related messages about the list (for example a confirmation), and to understand, in aggregate, whether emails are delivered, opened, or clicked. We do not sell your email address.

Who processes it

Email signups and sends are handled by MailerLite, which acts as our email service provider. Their processing is also covered by MailerLite’s own privacy policy. The site may be hosted by a static host such as Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, or GitHub Pages, which can see normal server logs as part of delivering the pages.

How long we keep it

We keep your email on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. You can unsubscribe with the link in every email.

Your choices

You can use the generator without subscribing. You can unsubscribe at any time, and you can email us to access, correct, or delete your signup information.

Cookies and analytics

This static site does not set its own marketing cookies. If we later add a simple analytics tool, this policy will be updated first. MailerLite may use cookies or similar technology as part of the signup form or to measure email engagement.

Children

DualGen is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their email addresses.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update the date above and, when the change affects how we use emails already on the list, we will say so in the newsletter.
