Shorts and long-form need different hooks

A YouTube Short hook has the same job as TikTok: stop the swipe. A long-form hook has to do one more thing — promise a payoff that is worth two, eight, or twenty minutes. DualGen's YouTube setting tells the prompt to choose a Short (30–45 seconds) or a long-form cold open (the first 20–30 seconds) and to say which one it wrote.

Use this if you make tutorials, reviews, vlogs, or talking-head explainers and the first line keeps coming out as “Hey guys.” The generator will still need your judgment. If the hook overclaims, rewrite it before you film.

A simple workflow

Generate three pairs. Read the hooks out loud. Keep the one you can say without grimacing. Paste the prompt into ChatGPT or Grok, then transfer the spoken lines into your usual script doc or teleprompter. Share the pair if you want a collaborator or future-you to land on the same result.
